# Blackout Hunt 0.9.0-beta.1

A big hardening pass before classroom play. We went hunting hard for anything that could wreck a live game — crashes, cheats, players getting stuck, rounds ending wrong — across the new movement, the train minigames, the anti-cheat, and the boot menu.

The verdict: the game's already solid. Nothing serious turned up. The couple of real things we found are fixed:

- **Sitting down** no longer leaves you looking glitched after you're caught or a round restarts, and you can't sit while mid-roll.
- The optional **anti-cheat** won't wrongly kick real students for normal play (like climbing out of a locker).

Plus a stack of behind-the-scenes build and packaging fixes so it cooks clean.

**Grab the zip, unzip, run `BlackoutHunt.exe`.** No account needed. Update everyone to this build — online play only matches other 0.9.0 copies (LAN and direct-IP work either way).
