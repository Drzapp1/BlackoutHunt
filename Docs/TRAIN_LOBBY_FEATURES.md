# Train Lobby Overhaul — feature guide

Branch: **`feat/train-lobby-games-comfort`** (kept separate from other agents' work). All gameplay is
server-authoritative and multiplayer-safe; every interactable uses the lobby's look-at + tap/hold verb.

## How to test
Close any running editor, then:
```
UnrealEditor.exe "D:\BlackoutHunt\BlackoutHunt.uproject" -game -windowed -resx=1600 -resy=900
```
Spawn into the lobby and walk the 15 cars. Core controls added: **C** = sit, **X** = emote, **E** = interact
(tap or hold), **O** = return to cabin (on the roof).

## The 15-car lobby (front → back)

7 Othello · 8 Blackjack · 9 Bar/Lounge · 10 Library (quiet) · 11 Observation deck · 12 Greenhouse ·

## Games (all server-authoritative; vs-AI or human PvP where noted)
- **Blackjack** — multi-seat: several players, each their own hand vs one dealer; your hand/chips show on your
  HUD. Tap to deal/hit, hold to stand.
- **Chess** — 6x6 fast minichess (R N B Q K R), capture-the-king. vs AI (negamax) or PvP. Each piece type has a
  distinct shape; the king wears a gold crown. Look at a square, tap to select/move, hold to deselect.
- **Tic-Tac-Toe** — vs perfect-AI or PvP.
- **Connect Four** — upright board, look at a column + tap to drop; alpha-beta AI or PvP.
- **Slots** — tap to spin for cosmetic credits (jackpot/diamonds/3-of-a-kind/pair).
- **Darts** — look-to-aim + tap to throw (with a little scatter); concentric ring scoring; per-player totals.
- **Othello / Reversi** — 8x8, look at a highlighted cell + tap to place and flip; vs AI (positional + mobility
  minimax) or PvP; HUD shows the board + disc counts.
Seating tip: at chess/connect-four/tic-tac-toe the first player is one colour, a second human is the other, or
**hold** to start vs the AI.

## Comfort / decompression
- **Functional sit** (C): camera lowers, movement locks, you stand on any move. Third-person pose hides the legs
  and drops the torso so others see you seated (no bespoke animation needed).
- **Pettable cats** that wander the relax cars (tap to pet — they sit and purr).
- **Aquariums** with drifting fish (tap the glass to startle them).
- **Fireplace** in the bar lounge — flickering flames + warm light (tap to light/douse).
- **Mood lighting**: coloured `APointLight` accents per themed car.
- **Vending machines** (bar + arcade): tap for a random free snack/drink — a can drops in the tray.
- Leather booths, reading chairs, planters, marble bar, etc., all using imported PBR materials.

## Socialization
- **Emotes** (X): cycles HI / GG / <3 / LOL / CHEERS / WOO — drawn as a bubble over your head for nearby players.
- **Jukebox** (karaoke car): tap to cycle looping music (spatialised). Plays the eerie lobby loop today; drop more
  CC0 loops into the playlist via import and they auto-appear.
- **Disco dance floor** (karaoke car): colour-cycling tiles; tap to change the pattern.
- Gathering spaces: bar, karaoke stage, multiplayer game tables.

## Easter eggs
- **Ghost passenger** — rarely materialises near the observation deck and fades away.
- **Secret switch** — hidden in the greenhouse back corner; flip it for a disco beacon + a party toast to everyone.

## Imported internet assets (CC0)

`BlackoutHunt/Tools/ImportTrainComfortMaterials.py` and exposed through `BHPropVisuals` + new `EBHBlockMaterial`

```
UnrealEditor.exe "<proj>.uproject" -ExecutePythonScript="...\Tools\ImportTrainComfortMaterials.py" -unattended -nosplash -nopause
```
(Use the full editor, NOT -Cmd; the `.uasset` are git-lfs and are regenerated by the script.)
